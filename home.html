Steps for Registration

📝 Step 1: Registration Details

Applicants can submit the following details:

  • Name - First name, Middle name, Last name, Verify name
  • Gender - Male, Female, Other
  • Date of Birth
  • Father name
  • Mother name
  • Phone no., Alternate phone no.
  • Valid email id
  • Board Examination, Roll no, Roll code, College name, etc.
  • Security Question

💻 Step 2: How to Apply Online

  • Candidate can be registered himself/herself
  • DTR (One Time Registration) posted by clicking the new Registration button
  • After Registration, he/she has to login (by email id/mobile number) to verify the already registered DTR Application
  • Apply for desirable Examination

🔐 Step 3: Login to Apply

Use your registered email or mobile number to login and complete your application for your desired examination.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Fill details exactly as per certificates
  • Use valid mobile number & email
  • Upload clear document (jpg/pdf)
  • Don't refresh during payment
  • Save application number for future
  • Check before submit - all the details
